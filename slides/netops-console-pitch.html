The problem

Past 500 subscribers,
the manual way starts costing you.

Four questions every growing ISP struggles to answer quickly — and each one has a price.

"Which port is free?"

Splitter capacity lives in a spreadsheet someone forgot to update. Nobody knows an ODP is full until the technician is already up the pole.

"Who is affected?"

One OLT goes dark and thirty alarms flood in. You learn the root cause from angry phone calls, not from your tools.

"Did they pay?"

Invoices in one app, PPPoE accounts in the router, suspension done by hand at midnight — and restored by hand the next morning.

"What happened last time?"

Customer history is scattered across WhatsApp threads, a paper ledger, and whatever a technician still remembers.

The solution

One console.
From the fiber to the invoice.

NetOps Console models the whole chain an ISP actually operates — OLT → ODC → ODP → subscriber — then layers monitoring, field work, customer self-service, and money on top of that single map.

Know the plant

Every OLT, splitter, cable run, and drop as real geometry on a vector-tile map — with optical loss budget along the path.

See it live

The server polls OLTs over SNMP directly. Time-series metrics in TimescaleDB, alarms correlated into incidents instead of noise.

Run the business

Packages, invoices, payment gateways, PPPoE sessions, and a branded customer portal — driven by the same subscriber record.
