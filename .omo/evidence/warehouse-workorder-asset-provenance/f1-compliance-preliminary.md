## Draft resource audit and required remediation

The term “saved draft” below means a DRAFT survives a successful transaction and can be left idle. A transient internal DRAFT followed by posting in the same transaction is different: expiry must never silently release picked reservations, receive transit stock or erase customer custody. No production idle-document expiration service, schedule, persisted timeout decision or test was found; only reservation and approval expiry exist. `docs/warehouse-contracts.md:335` says “Idle draft boleh expire tanpa stok” (may expire), which weakens plan C7 “Idle drafts expire without stock effects.” Documentation cannot narrow the approved requirement.
