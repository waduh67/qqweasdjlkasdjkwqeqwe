Original creation/replay replies are retained through different paths from current reads: disposition/compensation/loss return `record.view()` or the replayed record's view; return reacquisition returns `reference()`; supplier replacement returns `record.view`; migration opening returns `prior.body`; transfer discrepancy returns `prior.receipt`. Current expiry additions have nullable defaults and NON_NULL annotations on DTOs reused by saved records. No currentView/currentBody result is substituted into these replay returns.

Discrepancy recovery remains safe against an old request becoming effective after replacement. Both report/recovery and approval acquire the transfer before the resolution source. `WarehouseTransferApprovalOwner.validate/prepare` require the parent transfer revision and resolutionDocumentId to equal the request's original binding. The old request also fails the C7 source deadline checks; header/effect admission guards remain active. Recovery itself performs no movement, reservation release or custody change. A pending row may remain physically PENDING until the expiry worker/query cleanup, but it cannot authorize new effects after its source deadline.

Authorization paths were preserved in these changes: tenant-bound SQL and the existing visible-location filters remain; disposition/compensation lists retain per-item authorized current reads; return/replacement reads retain the original WO/return scope checks. The additional expiry metadata does not introduce an alternate lookup or an unscoped saved-snapshot route. This review does not assert a fresh audit of every pre-existing authorization path outside the requested deltas.

Relevant implementation locations are under `server/src/main/kotlin/com/duluin/ftth/inventory/adapter/outbound/persistence/`: `WarehouseDispositionStore.kt`, `WarehouseCompensationStore.kt`, `WarehouseAssetLossStore.kt`, `ReturnReacquisitionStore.kt`, `SupplierReplacementStore.kt`, `MigrationOpeningStore.kt`, `WarehouseTransferDiscrepancyStore.kt`, and `MaterialPlanningStore.kt`; their service/replay callers and DTOs are listed in the hash manifest.
