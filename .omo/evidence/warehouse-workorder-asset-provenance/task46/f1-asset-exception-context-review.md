## Required corrections

1. **Current customer scope on fresh POST and replay.** The new context correctly uses `customers.lockForException` (`S/inventory/application/service/InventoryAssetTitleService.kt:90`), but title creation still calls the old customer lock at line 44, and asset-loss creation at `WarehouseAssetLossService.kt:55`. That old owner adapter locks the customer but does not test its area. Both replay branches return before this late customer lock. Concrete sequence: obtain valid context in area A; move the customer into inaccessible area B while the original WO and warehouse locations remain in A; confirm the captured POST, or replay its previously committed key. Current command paths do not reauthorize that customer's area. The browser's preliminary GET is not a command authorization fence. These command methods predate this diff but are the newly exposed workflow's execution boundary.

   Preserve WO → topology → assignment → asset → customer order. Use the owner-scoped customer lock before fresh insertion and before returning any historical replay; authorize the saved customer/assignment for replay without requiring it to remain currently installed. Returning an allowed original replay must preserve its original body and must not post another effect. Denial after an area move must not mutate proposal/title/stock. Also add `access.location` for the current locked title position on fresh title creation: current context checks both the handover location and current asset location, whereas title POST currently only checks the former at line 34 before obtaining the latter at line 43.

   Parent accepted: add an asset lock that does not require active installation for historical replay, late customer-area reauthorization on both families, current title-position scope on fresh POST, and tests of fresh/old-key denial after customer area changes plus unchanged allowed replay bytes/effects.

2. **Loss requester without approval.view cannot hand the same proposal to another officer for submission.** `CustomerAssetPanel.tsx:29` and context `canRequestLoss` allow the source proposal with custody.manage + approval.request, without approval.view. `CustomerAssetException.tsx:51–52` then tells that creator to ask another authorized officer to continue. However, `S/inventory/application/service/DurableApprovalService.kt:38–39` requires approval.request and approval.view to submit an approval, and line 52 requires the actor to be the source's original requester. `WarehouseApprovalsPage.tsx:65–69` enforces the same UI semantics. The suggested handoff cannot work for that proposal.

   Minimum: explain that access must be granted to the original requester before they can continue; do not imply that another actor can submit their sealed source. Prefer withholding/disabling visible loss creation without approval.view and explaining that prerequisite. Do not broaden approval ownership or independent-review rules. Parent accepted that UI gate/explanation and a missing-view test. A final saved-state fallback still needs truthful wording if permission is revoked during the operation.
